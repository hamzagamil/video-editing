# Director's notes — game version

**Thesis:** «ميسي مش بيجري أسرع من المدافع.. ميسي عايش في المستقبل».
**Concept:** the reel is a video game and Messi is Player 1. The speaker's own words (كمبيوتر، ماتريكس، متأخر
بخطوة، فيديو جيم، NPCs) become the world from the first cut, so the last line pays off a world the viewer has
been inside all along.

**One rule for motion:** P1 moves early, crisp, in quick steps; NPCs move late and heavy; cyan "future" lines
always appear *before* the thing they predict (g6 aim assist, g9 NPC script, cw4/cw5 ghosts).
